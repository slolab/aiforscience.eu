# DFG Code of Conduct: Guidelines for Safeguarding Good Research Practice (2019)

!!! info "Reference"
    **Citation:** Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft. *Guidelines for Safeguarding Good Research Practice. Code of Conduct.* Bonn, 2019; Guideline 17, Archiving. **Type:** research-integrity code, binding on institutions that receive DFG funding. **Link:** [Guideline 17 (wissenschaftliche-integritaet.de)](https://wissenschaftliche-integritaet.de/en/code-of-conduct/archiving/).

## What it is

The German research funder's code of good research practice.
Guideline 17 asks researchers to back up research data, results, central materials, and research software, and to archive research data "in an accessible and identifiable manner for a period of ten years" at their institution or in a cross-location repository, counted from the date results are made public.
Institutions must provide the infrastructure for this.

## Role in the record

- Grounds [BP07](../best-practices/07-provenance-and-citation.md) (bp7-a1): research materials must stay available for audit for a fixed period, which a provider's chat history does not guarantee.
- Limits: a German funder's code; other countries and funders set their own retention periods, but the duty to keep research records is common to research-integrity codes.
