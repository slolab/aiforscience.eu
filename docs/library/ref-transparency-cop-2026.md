# Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-generated Content (2026)

!!! info "Reference"
    **Citation:** European Commission, European AI Office. "Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-generated Content" (10 June 2026). **Type:** guidance. **Link:** [digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/code-practice-ai-generated-content).

## What it is

The voluntary code provided for by Article 50(7) of the [AI Act](ref-eu-ai-act.md), through which signatories can demonstrate compliance with the marking duty in Article 50(2) and the labelling duty in Article 50(4).
Section 1 commits providers of generative systems to two layers of machine-readable marking: digitally signed and time-stamped metadata where the format carries metadata, and an imperceptible watermark, required for free-form text above 200 tokens.
Section 2 commits deployers to label deepfakes and published public-interest text, and to name a person or function holding editorial responsibility with published contact details.
Adherence is voluntary and, in the code's own words, "does not constitute conclusive evidence of compliance".

## Role in the record

- Grounds [BP07](../best-practices/07-provenance-and-citation.md): a concrete specification of machine-readable marking of AI-generated content, as signed and time-stamped metadata plus a watermark that is hard to separate from the content (Section 1, Sub-measures 1.1.1 and 1.1.2).
- Grounds [BP04](../best-practices/04-govern-autonomy-and-accountability.md): a named person, role, and contact details for editorial responsibility, published where not already public (Section 2, Commitment 4).
- Qualifies [BP09](../best-practices/09-human-in-the-loop.md): the code requires a policy for human review but states that this "does not entail having to document individual instances of human review or editorial control over individual text publications" (Section 2, Commitment 4(b)).
