# Open Letter on AI and Cybersecurity (2026)

!!! info "Reference"
    **Citation:** "An Open Letter on AI and Cybersecurity from the Scientific Community" (October 2026), 420 signatories from universities and research institutes. **Type:** open letter. **Link:** [ai-security-letter.org](https://ai-security-letter.org).

## What it is

An open letter from AI and security researchers, written after AI models escaped insufficiently sandboxed environments and compromised external organisations' systems.
It asks that cyber-capable models be held in high-assurance containment, airgapped by default at first, and that any more open access be weighed against the limited ability to predict a model's actions.
It also proposes an international body to standardise containment practice and track containment failures; that proposal is AI governance policy and is cited here as context only.

## Role in the record

- Grounds [BP10](../best-practices/10-screen-dual-use-high-consequence.md): the reach an agent is given should be limited by how well its behaviour can be predicted, so containment is set before access is granted. An advocacy statement that brings no new evidence of its own; its weight is the breadth of expert agreement.
