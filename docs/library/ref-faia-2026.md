# FAIA: FAIR AI Attribution White Paper (2026)

!!! info "Reference"

"FAIA - FAIR AI Attribution White Paper", version 0.9, project deliverable, Zenodo, 17 June 2026. **Type:** report (pre-publication white paper). **Link:** [doi:10.5281/zenodo.20083769](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20083769); framework site [faia.liccium.com](https://faia.liccium.com/).

## What it is

A framework for machine-readable, persistent, and verifiable disclosure of AI involvement in digital content, developed by Liccium with Leiden University and the GO FAIR Foundation.
It defines attribution flags (human-created, AI-assisted, AI-generated), activity codes for the AI's role in the content lifecycle, and optional identification of the AI system and version, declared in JSON-LD and bound to the content through ISCC fingerprints (ISO 24138:2024).
