# Library

Distilled source documents: strategy papers, policy reports, and institutional
documents relevant to agentic AI in science. Each entry summarises its source,
quotes the passages that matter with section and page locators, and maps them
to the [best practices](../best-practices/index.md).

Raw documents are not hosted here. Each entry carries a full citation to the
original.
