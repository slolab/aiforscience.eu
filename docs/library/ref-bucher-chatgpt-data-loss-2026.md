# Two years of academic work lost from a ChatGPT account (Bucher, Nature 2026)

!!! info "Reference"
    **Citation:** Bucher, M. "When two years of academic work vanished with a single click." *Nature*, career column, 22 January 2026. doi:10.1038/d41586-025-04064-7. **Type:** first-person account in a journal career column. **Link:** [doi.org/10.1038/d41586-025-04064-7](https://doi.org/10.1038/d41586-025-04064-7).

## What it is

A plant scientist at the University of Cologne describes using a ChatGPT Plus subscription daily to "write e-mails, draft course descriptions, structure grant applications, revise publications, prepare lectures, create exams and analyse student responses".
In August 2025 he switched off the account's "data consent" option, and "all of my chats were permanently deleted and the project folders were emptied".
He kept partial copies of some material; the rest could not be recovered.
The finished publications, lecture materials and e-mails survived; "What disappeared, however, was my entire underlying correspondence with ChatGPT: the prompts, follow-up questions, iterations and project folders."
He compares the loss to "losing the laboratory notebooks behind published research — the end results remain, but the developmental record and reusable building blocks are gone."
OpenAI, asked by Nature, agreed with his account and stated that deleted content "cannot be recovered via the user interface, application programming interfaces (APIs), or support", and: "For data protection, we always recommend users maintain personal backups for professional work."
His own conclusion: export important conversations regularly, keep systematic offline backups, and do not change data settings without knowing the consequences.

## Role in the record

- Grounds [BP07](../best-practices/07-provenance-and-citation.md) (bp7-a1): the process record behind published work (prompts, iterations, drafts) existed only in the provider's chat history, and once it was gone the path from the results back to how they were made could not be reconstructed.
- Recorded in the [Failures log](../best-practices/failures.md).
- Limits: a single first-person account by a private subscriber, and the loss followed a settings change, not a cancelled licence. The author reports that no warning appeared; OpenAI states that it shows a confirmation prompt before a chat is deleted.
