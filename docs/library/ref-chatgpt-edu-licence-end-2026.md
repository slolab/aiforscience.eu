# University guidance on ChatGPT Edu content when a licence ends (2026)

!!! info "Reference"
    **Citation:** Politecnico di Torino, "Activation of the ChatGPT EDU service for faculty and staff"; Washington University in St. Louis IT, "ChatGPT Edu"; San José State University IT, "ChatGPT Usage Eligibility". Institutional web pages, accessed 30 October 2026. **Type:** institutional IT guidance. **Links:** [Politecnico di Torino](https://it.polito.it/en/support/manuals/activation-of-the-chatgpt-edu-service-for-faculty-and-staff), [Washington University](https://it.washu.edu/openai-chatgpt-edu/), [San José State](https://www.sjsu.edu/it/services/applications/chatgpt/eligibility.php).

## What it is

Three universities tell staff and students what happens to their ChatGPT Edu content when access ends.
Politecnico di Torino: when the licence expires, the user "loses access to the Edu workspace and the associated content" (chats, files, Projects); "Content is not automatically transferred to any personal ChatGPT account", and "OpenAI does not allow a complete export of the data".
Washington University: "ChatGPT Edu users are currently unable to use ChatGPT's standard export feature", described as a vendor restriction; access is revoked "upon graduation, end of employment, or at the conclusion of WashU's contract with OpenAI Edu", after which "content follows a 30-day deletion schedule" and "Deleted chats are not recoverable."
San José State: "Data export is not possible", and there is no mechanism to warn users before their account is deleted.

## Role in the record

- Grounds [BP07](../best-practices/07-provenance-and-citation.md) (bp7-a1): when an institution's licence or a person's eligibility ends, work kept only in the provider's workspace is lost, and the institutional tier may offer no export at all.
- Limits: one provider and one product tier, at three universities; the pages describe policy, not a documented loss. Export options may change; each page states the position at the time of access.
