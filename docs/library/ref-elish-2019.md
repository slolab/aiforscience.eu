## What it is

An analysis of how responsibility falls on human operators in automated systems. The nominal human-in-the-loop can absorb blame for a system they cannot realistically control.

- Grounds [BP8](../best-practices/08-human-in-the-loop.md): a qualification on stated human-in-the-loop, showing the human can become a blame sink rather than an effective control.
